### Description · 4000
*Where:* the version page, iOS App
Usewell shows you what to use first, before it goes to waste.

Today lists the food that's closest to its date, and suggests 15-minute recipes that use it. Tap "I cooked it" and Usewell takes the food off your list.

Add food by typing it, or take a photo of your receipt: the receipt is read on your phone and never leaves it. A reminder the evening before tells you what needs using tomorrow.

Usewell is free. Usewell Plus, $2.99 a month, adds more recipes each day. Manage or cancel Plus at any time in your App Store settings.
