# The market check

The demand and competition check, run after the description is confirmed. Every claim has its source and the date it was read.

## Demand
Signals that people want this, each with its source and date.

## Competitors
The closest apps in a short table: what they do, price, rating, and what they miss.

## Verdict
One line: more demand and less competition, or not.

## Suggested tweaks
Up to three changes that would make the app easier to sell, and whether the user took them.

## Sources
Every page read, with the date.
