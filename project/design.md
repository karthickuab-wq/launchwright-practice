# The design guide

The short guide every screen follows, made from the style the user chose.

## The style chosen
Its name, and one line on why it fits this app.

## Colours
Each colour, what it's for, and its value.

## Type
The fonts and sizes for headings, body text and buttons.

## Spacing and shapes
The spacing steps, corner rounding and tap-target size (at least 44 pixels).

## Tone of voice
How the app talks to its users, with two or three examples.

## Do's and don'ts
What every screen does, and what it never does.
