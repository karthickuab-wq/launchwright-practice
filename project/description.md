# The app description

The confirmed app description: one page, always in these sections. Written at the end of the idea stage and confirmed by the user with a tap.

## Working name
The name to use until the user chooses the final one.

## The pitch
One line on what the app does and for whom.

## Who it's for, and the problem
The people it serves, and the problem it solves for them.

## What version 1 does
Three to five things, no more.

## What waits until later
Ideas that are good but not in version 1.

## How it makes money
Free, paid, a subscription, or something else, in the user's words.

## Where it runs
iPhone, Android, the web: whichever apply.

## Outside services
Each service the app will need, such as payments or email, and what for.

## Open questions
Anything still undecided. Empty once the description is confirmed, or each item says who decides it and when.
