## Verdict
Real demand with paying users, and a leader that is expensive and complex for one-person businesses. Win by being the simple, cheap before-and-after report app for solo tradespeople (cleaners, painters, handymen).

## Suggested tweaks
1. Aim at solo tradespeople, not teams: CompanyCam's price is per user and its reviews ask for a simpler version. Taken: not yet.
2. Make the client report the whole product: one tap from before/after photos to a branded report link. Why: the Nov 2026 reviews show reports are what users depend on. Taken: not yet.
