## Verdict
Weak demand, and the book idea has been done at least four times since 2017 with no sign of big sales. It can only earn if it spreads on social media; it's a fun side project, not a likely earner.

## Suggested tweaks
1. Test with something small before an app: a few dozen share-ready parody verses posted online, to see if people share them. Why: every Trump humor app found is at 1K+ downloads or fewer. Taken: not yet.
2. Make the side-by-side "real verse, then Trump's version" the hook. Why: the books found replace the text with rants; none shown in search results pairs them. (From search results only.) Taken: not yet.
